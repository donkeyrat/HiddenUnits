
# Hidden Units

![enter image description here](https://imgur.com/C6L1cOi.png)
(Incredible splash art by Hambody)

__EIGHTY THREE__ different unique units are added by this mod, a majority of which can be found in the Hidden Units faction. *HOWEVER*, all units in this faction must be unlocked, just like ordinary secret units!

In addition to secret units, this mod also comes with TWO new factions, the **Steampunk** and **Egypt** factions! These factions each include **seven high-quality units**. Steampunk has one map, and Egypt has two, each complete with a set of incredible music tracks composed by the legendary Whisperjas!

All of the Steampunk and Egypt secret units can be found on their respective maps. Each faction also has a campaign.

__ALL OF THE CLOTHING, WEAPONS, AND ABILITIES ARE AVAILABLE IN THE UNIT CREATOR.__

## How To Use

*The Egypt/Steampunk faction and maps are unlocked upon loading the game with the mod for the first time.*

To unlock the secret units, you'll have to find them first.

## Steampunk Units

| Unit | Description |
|-|-|
| **Automaton** | A mass-produced machine designed to do manual labor for the elite, the Automatons use their snapping claws to drag units to the ground, rendering them vulnerable to attack. |
| **Metrocop** | The Metrocop police force bravely protect the streets of the Steampunk city. They do so with their stun batons, tasing enemies as they approach before whacking them with the baton. |
| **Mechanic** | Mechanics are tinkerers with a passion for invention. Their proudest combat invention is the bird drone, piloted through a remote they hold in their hands. These birds target the furthest enemy from the Mechanic, bullying the enemy backline. |
| **Magnetic Rod** | Created by the famous inventor Nikola Tesla, the Magnetic Rods are towers of copper and electricity, utilizing a mix of steam and future technologies to harness magnetic power. The magnet atop the tower can pull in enemies, disabling the use of their weapons as they're dragged. |
| **Bounty Hunter** | Bounty Hunters patrol the streets in search of the most dangerous and wanted criminals. Equipped with a cog sniper, the deadly bullets of the Bounty Hunter deal bonus damage to units with high health pools. |
| **Powerminer** | The great miners of the coal depths provide power to the entire city, though their work is no simple task. They wield two large drills with their sturdy exosuit, drilling through enemies and flinging them around. The exosuit they wear also comes with protective plating, granting the miners great resilience until the armor chips off and breaks. |
| ***IRON BEHEMOTH*** | A colossal machine build by the illusive Clockwork Mages, the Iron Behemoth stands tall above all other units and even the city itself. With three smokestack cannons at the back, it fires barrages of airstrikes onto enemy armies, dealing explosive damage. When in close range, it fires bullets from two mounted turrets, mowing down the enemy army. |
